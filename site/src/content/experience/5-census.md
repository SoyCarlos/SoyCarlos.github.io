  &bull; Developed data science tools to support the U.S. Census Bureau's frame maintenance and data quality processes.  
  &bull; Designed and implemented a probabilistic record-matching system using natural language processing techniques to automate tasks previously performed manually.  
  &bull; Improved efficiency and accuracy of frame matching workflows by applying tokenization, vectorization, and similarity scoring methods.  
  &bull; Built web crawlers and scrapers in Python to automatically identify and extract relevant information from public websites.  
  &bull; Collaborated with federal staff to understand operational requirements, data constraints, and regulatory considerations.  
  &bull; Documented methodologies and presented results to technical and non-technical stakeholders within the agency.  
  &bull; Participated in the Civic Digital Fellowship program, working alongside other fellows to modernize public-sector technology.
